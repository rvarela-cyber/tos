WHO WE ARE

The Dock, The Fleet, The Crews. All Under One Roof To Serve Your Operations.

Move Solutions is part of the Total Office Solutions family of companies: Move Solutions, Office Furniture Plus, Office Boy, Tech Team Solutions, and Paint & Carpet One. Together we operate one of the largest commercial moving, warehousing, and FF&E operations in the Southwest, with over 400,000 square feet of warehouse, showroom, and staging space across DFW.

That scale is why we can do what smaller cross-dock operators can't: run around-the-clock crews, hold 8 trailers at a time when the weather hits, receive 60 sporadic parcel deliveries for a national pop-up, and still have the fleet, labor, and gated yard ready for the next trailer that pulls up.

As a founding member of the Office Moving Alliance (OMA), our reach extends well beyond DFW through 45+ partner companies in 228 markets nationwide. Local in Dallas. National when you need it.

Quick Answers.

What hours are you open?
24/7/365. Overnight crews, weekend crews, after-hours crews on payroll. No voicemail.
Do I need an appointment?
No lead time required for authorized carriers and brokers. Call dispatch, pull up to the gate.
What do you charge?
Pricing is handled through dispatch or your broker. We don't automatically add a $175 after-hours fee.
What can't you handle?
Dry goods only. We do not handle frozen, refrigerated, hazmat, alcohol (TABC), or long-term cold storage.
How do you protect my freight?
Gated yard, overnight watchman, 24/7 cameras, lock-and-key secured zones for high-value freight.
