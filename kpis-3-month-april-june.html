How We Get To These Numbers

Every result gets a price tag based on what that kind of lead actually costs to buy on the open market in 2026. We pull those prices from published industry benchmarks (First Page Sage, Belkins, Martal and others) for our field, then multiply how many we got by the price and add it up.
